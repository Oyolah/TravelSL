Tips for Working with Local Operators

  • Always confirm prices and services in writing before booking
  • Ask for references or reviews from previous travelers
  • Verify that operators have valid licenses from the National Tourist Board
  • Book airport transfers in advance, especially for late arrivals
  • Tipping is appreciated - 10-15% for guides and drivers is customary
