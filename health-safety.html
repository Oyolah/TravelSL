Consult a travel health clinic 4-6 weeks before your trip for personalized medical advice.

Health Tips

Water Safety

Drink only bottled or boiled water. Avoid ice in drinks and use bottled water for brushing teeth. Bottled water is widely available.

Mosquito Protection

Use DEET-based insect repellent, wear long sleeves at dusk, and sleep under mosquito nets. Malaria is the biggest health risk.

Sun Protection

Apply high SPF sunscreen regularly, wear a hat, and stay hydrated. The tropical sun is strong, especially between 10am-4pm.

Food Safety

Eat at reputable restaurants, ensure food is cooked thoroughly, and avoid raw vegetables that may have been washed in tap water.

Safety Tips

  • Keep valuables secure and avoid displaying expensive jewelry, cameras, or electronics in crowded areas.
  • Make copies of important documents (passport, visa, insurance) and store them separately from originals.
  • Register with your country's embassy in Freetown and share your itinerary with family or friends.
  • Be cautious at the beach - strong currents can be dangerous. Swim only in areas where locals swim.
  • Purchase comprehensive travel insurance that covers medical evacuation before your trip.
  • Respect local customs and dress modestly, especially when visiting religious sites or rural communities.
