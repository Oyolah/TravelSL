Our Story

Visit Freetown is dedicated to promoting tourism in Sierra Leone's beautiful capital city and beyond.

Founded with a passion for showcasing West Africa's hidden gem, we aim to connect travelers with the rich culture, stunning beaches, vibrant history, and warm hospitality that make Freetown truly special.

From the iconic Cotton Tree to the pristine beaches of the Peninsula, we're here to help you discover all that Sierra Leone has to offer.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To promote sustainable tourism in Sierra Leone by providing authentic experiences, supporting local communities, and showcasing the natural beauty and cultural richness of Freetown and its surroundings.

Our Vision

To establish Sierra Leone as a premier tourist destination in West Africa, recognized for its pristine beaches, vibrant culture, warm hospitality, and commitment to sustainable tourism practices.
